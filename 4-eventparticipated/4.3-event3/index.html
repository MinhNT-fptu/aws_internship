Summary of the Event and Lessons Learned

1. Overview of FCAJ - AGENTIC AI BUILD WEEK

Program Information

FCAJ - AGENTIC AI BUILD WEEK is a program for people who are interested in artificial intelligence, particularly Agentic AI. The event provides participants with an opportunity to learn about, build, and develop AI solutions in a practical, creative, and collaborative environment.

Main Content

The event focused on applying Agentic AI to solve real-world problems.

Unlike conventional chatbots that mainly respond to user requests, AI agents can receive goals, analyze tasks, create plans, and perform multiple steps to complete their assigned objectives.

An AI agent can perform activities such as:

  • Receiving and analyzing user requests.
  • Dividing a large goal into smaller tasks.
  • Creating a step-by-step action plan.
  • Using external tools or APIs.
  • Retrieving and processing data.
  • Evaluating results and adjusting its actions.
  • Collaborating with other AI agents to complete tasks.

Through the event, attendees had the opportunity to observe how participating teams developed their ideas, selected problems, built prototypes, and presented their solutions.

Lessons Learned

I gained a clearer understanding of Agentic AI and the differences between AI agents and conventional chatbots.

An effective Agentic AI product requires more than a powerful AI model. It also needs a clearly defined objective, a logical workflow, suitable data, and the ability to connect with external tools.

2. Benefits of Participating in a Hackathon

Main Content

A hackathon is an activity in which participants work individually or in teams to build a product within a limited period of time.

Participating in a hackathon offers many benefits related to knowledge, skills, and career development.

First, a hackathon provides participants with an opportunity to apply theoretical knowledge to real-world problems. Instead of learning about technology only through documents and tutorials, participants must directly analyze a problem, select an appropriate solution, and build a working prototype.

Hackathons also improve problem-solving abilities. Within a short period, teams must identify important requirements, prioritize features, and handle difficulties that arise during product development.

Participants can also develop skills such as:

  • Analyzing and identifying problems.
  • Developing creative ideas.
  • Planning the implementation process.
  • Working effectively in a team.
  • Assigning responsibilities.
  • Managing time.
  • Presenting and defending ideas.
  • Receiving feedback from experts.
  • Rapidly building a functional prototype.

Lessons Learned

A hackathon is not only a programming competition but also an environment where participants can learn, experiment, and develop themselves.

The most valuable outcome of a hackathon is not necessarily winning a prize. The process of building a product, collaborating with teammates, and solving practical problems also provides valuable experience.

3. Developing Teamwork Skills

Main Content

Most hackathon projects are completed by teams. Each team member usually takes responsibility for a different role, such as frontend development, backend development, artificial intelligence, interface design, business analysis, or product presentation.

To complete a product within a limited period, team members must agree on common goals, communicate regularly, and coordinate their work effectively.

A hackathon team needs to perform activities such as:

  • Defining the project’s common goals.
  • Selecting an idea that is relevant to the event’s theme.
  • Assigning tasks based on each member’s abilities.
  • Monitoring project progress.
  • Supporting team members when they encounter difficulties.
  • Integrating different components into a complete product.
  • Preparing the product presentation and demonstration.

Without effective communication, teams may experience duplicated work, missing features, or difficulty integrating the product before the deadline.

Lessons Learned

Teamwork plays an important role in a hackathon.

A team may have an excellent idea but still fail to complete the product if its members do not coordinate effectively. In contrast, a team that assigns responsibilities appropriately and supports one another can achieve good results within a short period.

4. Improving Time and Product Scope Management

Main Content

Hackathons usually limit the amount of time available for product development. Therefore, participants cannot build every possible feature from the beginning. They must identify the most important parts of their proposed solution.

Teams often develop their products as a Minimum Viable Product – MVP. An MVP is a version of a product that includes enough core functionality to demonstrate that the idea can work.

The process of building an MVP may include:

  1. Identifying the problem that needs to be solved.
  2. Identifying the target users.
  3. Proposing an appropriate solution.
  4. Selecting the most important features.
  5. Building a prototype or experimental product.
  6. Testing the primary user flow.
  7. Preparing the product demonstration.
  8. Presenting the value of the product.

Managing the project scope allows teams to focus on essential functions and avoid spending too much time on unnecessary details.

Lessons Learned

In a project with limited time, prioritizing the right tasks is more important than developing too many features.

A demonstration product does not need to be as complete as a commercial product. However, it must clearly present the problem, the proposed solution, the target users, and the value that the product provides.

5. Expanding Knowledge of Agentic AI

Main Content

By attending the event, I was introduced to different potential applications of Agentic AI.

AI agents can be used in areas such as:

  • Customer support.
  • Education and training.
  • Data analysis.
  • Task management.
  • E-commerce.
  • Finance.
  • Healthcare.
  • Cybersecurity.
  • Software development.
  • Business process automation.

An Agentic AI system may include the following components:

  • Large Language Model: Analyzes requests and generates responses.
  • Memory: Stores information and interaction history.
  • Planning: Creates plans to accomplish assigned objectives.
  • Tools: Connects to APIs, databases, or external services.
  • Knowledge Base: Provides relevant information and knowledge to the AI agent.
  • Evaluation: Assesses the quality of the generated results.
  • Guardrails: Controls the system’s behavior and helps ensure safety.

Combining these components allows AI agents to perform more complex tasks than conventional chatbots.

Lessons Learned

When building an AI agent, developers need to consider the entire system workflow instead of focusing only on the language model.

Factors such as data quality, tool usage, memory management, security, and result evaluation have a major influence on the reliability of an Agentic AI product.

6. Developing Creative and Problem-Solving Thinking

Main Content

Hackathons encourage participants to identify real-world problems and propose innovative solutions.

A good idea does not necessarily need to use the most complicated technology. It needs to solve a specific problem and provide clear value to its users.

When developing an idea, participants should answer questions such as:

  • What is the current problem?
  • Who is affected by the problem?
  • What are the limitations of existing solutions?
  • How can Agentic AI help solve the problem?
  • What benefits does the product provide?
  • Is the proposed solution practical?
  • Can the product continue to be developed after the event?

This process helps participants develop a user-centered mindset and avoid building a product simply because a new technology is available.

Lessons Learned

Technology only becomes valuable when it is used to solve an appropriate problem.

Before developing a product, it is necessary to understand the users’ needs, identify the product’s core value, and evaluate the feasibility of the proposed solution.

7. Learning and Networking Opportunities

Main Content

A hackathon brings together people who share an interest in technology, innovation, and product development.

Participants may have opportunities to meet:

  • Students from different universities.
  • Software developers.
  • Designers.
  • Data specialists.
  • Artificial intelligence engineers.
  • Mentors and technology experts.
  • Business representatives.
  • Recruiters.
  • People developing start-up projects.

Communicating with participating teams and experts allows attendees to discover different perspectives, learn from product development experiences, and stay updated on emerging technologies.

Hackathons may also create opportunities for future collaboration on personal projects, academic projects, or professional work.

Lessons Learned

Networking is one of the important benefits of attending a technology event.

Actively communicating, asking questions, and sharing ideas allows participants to learn more while also developing relationships that may support their academic and professional development.

8. Improving Product Presentation Skills

Main Content

In addition to building a product, participating teams must present their solutions within a limited period of time.

A hackathon presentation usually includes:

  • The problem that needs to be solved.
  • The target users.
  • The proposed idea and solution.
  • The application of Agentic AI.
  • The system architecture or operational workflow.
  • The main product features.
  • A product demonstration.
  • The practical value of the solution.
  • Opportunities for future development and expansion.

Presenters need to communicate clearly, focus on the product’s most important features, and help the audience understand the value of their solution.

A product may have excellent features, but an unclear presentation can make it difficult for judges and attendees to recognize its strengths.

Lessons Learned

Presentation skills are as important as product development skills.

During a presentation, teams should focus on the problem, the value of the proposed solution, and the product demonstration instead of only describing the technologies used.

Conclusion

Attending FCAJ - AGENTIC AI BUILD WEEK helped me gain a better understanding of Agentic AI, the process of building a product during a hackathon, and the skills required to develop a technological solution.

Through the event, I learned that hackathons provide many benefits. They allow participants to apply their knowledge to practical situations, improve teamwork, manage time effectively, solve problems, develop presentation skills, and expand their professional networks.

Although I attended the event as an observer, I still had the opportunity to watch how teams developed their ideas, built their products, and presented their solutions. This experience provided valuable knowledge that can help me prepare for participating in hackathons in the future.

The event also helped me understand that the success of an AI product does not depend only on technology. It also depends on correctly identifying the problem, understanding the users, and developing a solution with practical value.
